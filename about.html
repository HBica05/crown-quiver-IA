About the Club

Who We Are

Crown & Quiver is a proposed archery club designed for the Asia Pacific University community.

The club aims to create a welcoming environment where students can learn, practise and enjoy archery together, regardless of their previous experience.

We believe archery is more than a sport. It can help develop concentration, discipline, patience, confidence and respect for others.

“Aim with focus. Grow with confidence. Belong to a community.”

Club Experience

What We Offer

Crown & Quiver is designed to provide members with opportunities to learn, practise and participate in a supportive archery environment.

01

Beginner Training

Introductory sessions can help new members understand archery safety, basic shooting technique and correct use of equipment.

02

Regular Practice

Members can participate in planned practice sessions designed to improve consistency, accuracy and confidence.

03

Events & Competitions

Friendly competitions, workshops and club activities can give members the opportunity to challenge themselves and connect with others.

Our Community

Join Our Community

Crown & Quiver welcomes students who are interested in sport, personal growth, teamwork and becoming part of a supportive campus community.

Previous archery experience is not required. The proposed club is intended to provide opportunities for both beginners and students who already have experience with the sport.
