Equipment Basics

Understand Your Gear

Archery equipment should be used carefully and correctly. Understanding each item helps members practise safely and develop better shooting technique.

Beginners do not need to own every piece of equipment before learning the sport. Club equipment can support introductory training and supervised practice.

“Good technique begins with safe and responsible equipment use.”

Equipment Guide

These are some of the main items members may use during Crown & Quiver training and activities.

01

Recurve Bow

The standard bow used for beginner training and Olympic-style archery.

02

Arrows

Arrows are designed for safe target shooting and consistent flight.

03

Target Face

Targets are used for scoring and accuracy practice during training sessions.

04

Arm Guard

Protects the archer's forearm from the bowstring during shooting.

05

Finger Tab

Protects the fingers and helps create a smoother string release.

06

Quiver

Holds arrows securely and allows easy access during practice.

Equipment Safety

Check Before You Shoot

Bows, arrows and protective equipment should be checked before each session. Damaged equipment should not be used and should be reported to the responsible coach or club officer.

Inspect. Prepare. Shoot safely.
