Eligibility

Who Can Join?

Previous archery experience is not required. Members simply need an interest in learning, participating and following club safety standards.

  • Registered APU student.
  • No previous archery experience required.
  • Willing to follow club rules and safety regulations.
  • Able to attend training sessions regularly.
  • Respect fellow members and club equipment.
