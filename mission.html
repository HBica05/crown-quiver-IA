Our Direction

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Discover the purpose and direction behind Crown & Quiver and the values that guide the proposed APU Archery Club.

Our Mission

Developing Archers Through Discipline and Community

Our mission is to promote archery as a safe, disciplined and enjoyable sport within the university community.

Crown & Quiver aims to provide quality training, encourage sportsmanship, support personal growth and create opportunities for students to develop confidence, focus and teamwork through archery.

The club is designed to create an inclusive environment where every member can feel welcomed, respected and encouraged to develop their potential.

“Focus on the target, respect the journey and grow together.”

Crown & Quiver

Our Vision

A Strong and Supportive Archery Community

Crown & Quiver aims to become a welcoming university archery community where students can develop sporting skills, confidence, discipline and meaningful connections.

01

Inclusive

Create an environment where beginners and experienced archers can participate comfortably and respectfully.

02

Skill Focused

Encourage members to develop safe shooting technique, consistency and confidence through structured practice.

03

Community Driven

Build teamwork, friendship and sportsmanship through shared training, workshops and club activities.

Our Objectives

What We Aim to Achieve

The proposed club is guided by practical objectives focused on safety, learning, participation and personal development.

01

Promote Safety

Ensure that members understand and follow proper range rules, equipment handling and archery safety procedures.

02

Develop Skills

Provide opportunities for members to learn and improve archery technique, accuracy and equipment awareness.

03

Encourage Participation

Organise training sessions, workshops, friendly events and competitions that encourage active member involvement.

04

Build Confidence

Support personal growth by helping members develop patience, concentration, discipline and self-confidence.

05

Strengthen Teamwork

Encourage members to communicate, cooperate and support one another during training and club activities.

06

Grow the Community

Create an enjoyable club environment where students can connect through a shared interest in archery.
