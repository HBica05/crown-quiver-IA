Safety First

Club Rules & Safety Guidelines

Safe behaviour, respect and responsible equipment use are essential parts of every Crown & Quiver activity.

Range Safety

Safety Comes Before Every Shot

Archery requires concentration, discipline and careful behaviour. Members are expected to follow safety instructions at all times during training, workshops and club events.

These rules are intended to help create a safe, respectful and enjoyable environment for everyone using the range.

“Respect the range. Respect the equipment. Respect each other.”

Crown & Quiver Safety Principle

Club Guidelines

10 Essential Rules

Every member should understand and follow these guidelines before participating in archery activities.

01

Follow Instructions

Always follow the instructions of coaches and club officers.

02

Never Aim at People

Never point a bow at another person, even when no arrow is attached.

03

Shoot Only When Safe

Only shoot when the range has been declared safe.

04

Wait Before Collecting

Wait for the signal before collecting arrows from the target area.

05

Inspect Equipment

Inspect equipment before use and report any damage immediately.

06

Wear Suitable Attire

Wear suitable sports attire and closed-toe shoes during practice.

07

Show Respect

Respect other members, coaches and university facilities.

08

Avoid Unsafe Behaviour

Horseplay, running and distracting behaviour are not allowed on the range.

09

Return Equipment Properly

Return club equipment properly after each session.

10

Practise Sportsmanship

Maintain good sportsmanship during training and competitions.
